Why does WhatsApp blur my photos?

WhatsApp automatically compresses every image to save bandwidth. When your photo is too large (over 5 MB), it gets crushed to a fraction of its quality. The fix is simple: compress the photo before you send it, at settings WhatsApp won't touch again.

PicLight's WhatsApp preset shrinks your photo to 1280px wide and around 200–500 KB — the sweet spot where WhatsApp leaves the image alone. No blurring. No visible quality loss.

Unlike every other compressor online, PicLight never uploads your photo to a server. The compression happens inside your own browser using the same Canvas technology that powers games and photo editors. Your passport scans, family photos, and documents stay completely private.
